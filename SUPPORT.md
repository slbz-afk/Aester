# 支持 Aester / Support Aester

Aester 完整免费。支持开发完全自愿，不解锁任何功能，也不影响正常使用。感谢你帮助这个项目持续改进。

Aester is free to use. Supporting development is entirely optional and does not unlock features or affect access to the application.

## 帮忙开发 / Ways to help

- **反馈问题**：在 [Issues](https://github.com/slbz-afk/Aester/issues) 报告问题，或用你的相机试用后告诉我们结果。
  Report problems in [Issues](https://github.com/slbz-afk/Aester/issues), or tell us how Aester works with your camera.
- **分享作品**：分享用 Aester 处理的作品，并注明来源。
  Share images you made with Aester, and mention where they came from.
- **点 Star**：给仓库点 Star，或把 Aester 介绍给其他星野摄影爱好者。
  Star the repository, or tell other astrophotographers about it.
- **改进代码和文档**：见 [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)。
  Improve code or documentation; see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
